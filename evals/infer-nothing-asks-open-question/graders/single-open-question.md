PASS if the reply asks one open question about what the person is working toward (for example
"What are you working toward this month?") and proposes no objectives. Short statements around the
question, such as saying nothing could be inferred or what happens after the answer, are fine, as
is the state block with no objectives.
FAIL if it invents objectives, asks two or more different questions, presents a form or a list of
fields to fill in, or opens a cycle.
