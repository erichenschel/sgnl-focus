# Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

The SGNL plugin is a set of written instructions for an AI assistant. It has no server, no
scripts, no analytics, and no network calls.

- **Collected:** nothing. The plugin's authors receive no data from you or about you.
- **Stored:** nothing. The plugin keeps no record. The state block it writes stays in your own
  conversation, and you decide whether to copy it anywhere.
- **Sent:** nothing. The plugin sends no data to the authors or to any third party.

The skill does not ask for health details, account numbers, government or other IDs, or
credentials, and it leaves them out of the state block if you offer them.

What you type is processed by the assistant platform you use the plugin in. That platform's own
privacy policy and settings apply to your conversations, including retention and training
controls.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/erichenschel/sgnl-plugin/issues. Do not post
personal information in an issue; issues are public.
